## 2. How the affiliate program works
Source: [12 Aug kickoff](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-08-12-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-kickoff.md) and [3 Sep deep dive](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-09-03-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-deep-dive.md).
1. Tomas finds creators who already post food or baking content, 5K to 100K followers, and sends Dana a list.
2. Dana approves each creator, within 48 hours of getting the list.
3. One sample box per approved creator, and only after they accept the targeted plan.
4. Creators post. Commission is paid through TikTok Shop: 15% on the open plan, 20% on the targeted plan for the top 10 (the 12 Aug kickoff).
5. Every Wednesday, Marcus sends Dana the affiliates summary: new affiliates, posts, affiliate GMV. She called it "very useful" on 22 Sep and asked for one addition: **which creators drove the most sales**, from 23 Sep.
